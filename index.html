ClassRide

A smart transportation management platform that simplifies university bus transportation by bringing students, drivers, bus owners, and administrators together in one centralized system.

The Problem

Traditional transportation management often relies on WhatsApp messages, spreadsheets, and paper records, making it difficult to coordinate applications, manage trips, track attendance, and communicate efficiently.

Key Features
  • Separate dashboards and role-based access for students, drivers, bus owners, and admins
  • Student applications and matching with transportation providers
  • Trip management and attendance tracking
  • GPS-based location and estimated arrival information
  • In-app communication and notifications
  • Trip time management and authorized schedule adjustments
